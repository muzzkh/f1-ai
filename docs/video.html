This is measured on footage the project renders itself. Real broadcast video uses a different on-screen design, so it needs its own layout profile, calibrated in the dashboard. That has not been tested yet.

How it reads

Four steps, all running on an ordinary laptop.

01

Know where to look

A layout profile gives the position of every graphic as a fraction of the frame, so the same profile works at any resolution.

02

Read the numerals

A small neural network, trained on digits rendered from the fonts on this machine with blur, noise and slant added, reads speed and gear.

03

Measure the gauges

Throttle, brake and revs are read by how far the colour of each gauge extends around its arc.

04

Reject the impossible

A car cannot change speed by 80 km/h between two frames. Readings that imply it are thrown out and filled from their neighbours.
